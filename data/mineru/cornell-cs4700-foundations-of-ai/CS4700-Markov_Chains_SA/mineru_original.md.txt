Consider the following “random walker” in hypercube space:

1) Start at a random node S (the “current node”). (How do we generate such a node?)

2) Select, at random, one of the N neighbors of S, call it S'

3) If $(\mathrm{f}(\mathrm{S}') - \mathrm{f}(\mathrm{S})) > 0$, move to $\mathrm{S}'$, i.e. set $\mathrm{S} := \mathrm{S}'$ (i.e., jump to node with better value) else with probability $\mathrm{e}^{\wedge}(\mathrm{f}(\mathrm{S}') - \mathrm{f}(\mathrm{S})) / \mathrm{T}$ move to $\mathrm{S}'$, i.e., set $\mathrm{S} := \mathrm{S}'$

$$
\begin{array}{r l} {\mathrm{Prob(S)} = \mathrm{e} ^ {\wedge} (\mathrm{f(S)/T}) / \mathrm{Z}} & {= \frac {\mathrm{e} ^ {\mathrm{f(S')/T}}}{\mathrm{e} ^ {\mathrm{f(S)/T}}}} \\ & {= \frac {\mathrm{e} ^ {\mathrm{f(S')/T}}}{\mathrm{e} ^ {\mathrm{f(S)/T}}} \quad \textcircled {2}} \\ & {= \frac {\mathrm{prob(S')}}{\mathrm{prob(S)}} \quad \textcircled {*} *} \end{array}
$$

## So, we 'jump' based on ratio of decay probability.
