## 4 First Order Logic

Match the formula to the English sentence it encodes (note that there are more English sentences than formulas). Let  $Big(x)$  mean “x is big” (so  $\neg Big(x)$  means “x is small”) and let  $Cat(x)$  mean “x is a cat.”

1. $\exists x.Big(x)\wedge Cat(x)$ A. All cats are big.

2. $\exists x.Big(x)\to Cat(x)$ B. There is a small thing or a cat.

3. $\exists x.Big(x)\lor Cat(x)$ C. All big things are cats.

4. $\forall x.Big(x)\wedge Cat(x)$ D. Everything is a big cat.

5. $\forall x.Big(x)\to Cat(x)\quad \mathrm{E}.$ All cats are small.

6. $\forall x.Big(x)\lor Cat(x)$ F. There is a big cat.

G. All small things are cats.

H. There is a big thing or a cat.
